<p>Archiv is an Android app for managing and scanning documents, featuring fast search, tagging, and local processing for maximum privacy.</p>

<p><strong>Features</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>High-Quality Scanning</strong>: Capture documents with precision and ML-powered corner detection.</li>
	<li><strong>Document Management</strong>: Organize your scanned documents with a powerful tagging system and fast search.</li>
	<li><strong>PDF Generation</strong>: Convert your scans into high-quality PDF files.</li>
	<li><strong>Privacy First</strong>: All processing and storage happen locally on your device.</li>
	<li><strong>Multi-language Support</strong>: Available in English and Hindi.</li>
	<li><strong>Modern UI</strong>: Built with Jetpack Compose for a smooth and responsive experience.</li>
</ul>

<p>Everything is processed on-device, and your documents never leave your phone. Open-source and privacy-focused.</p>
